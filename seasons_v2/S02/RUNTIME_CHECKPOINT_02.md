## Governance shift — latest persistent decision
The player selected:
1. Reaffirm the original B-house pump trade: the farm that supplied equipment receives only a right to be consulted first when surplus water exists; no guaranteed priority water right.
2. Do not convert contribution level into priority rights over critical survival resources such as water. Contributions may be compensated in other areas such as transport, electricity support, barter terms, or labor exchange.
3. Delegate future management of contribution claims, water-use disputes, and similar village coordination to the village headman rather than the player's household.
4. Keep the headman's coordination role sustainable through a separately agreed, transparent and reasonable support/compensation arrangement discussed with well-owning households; do not make it a secret kickback or personal control arrangement.

Rationale expressed by player:
- These disputes will continue recurring.
- Direct management by the player's household would consume legitimacy, manpower, and emotional energy.
- Influence should be institutional/group-based rather than concentrated personally in 준호.
