# AM task

1. Call `archive_knowledge_semantic_job_current()` once. If it returns `NO_JOB`, stop quietly.
2. Work only on the returned `job_id`, source identity, compact context, and pinned policy. Read only the supplied public-safe source references needed to understand the source.
3. Decide whether there is one distinct, useful Knowledge question. Compare meaning against the supplied existing candidates and briefs. Risk determines disposition, not whether a worthwhile question is preserved: a strong high-risk question belongs in HUMAN_REVIEW rather than being discarded only because it is not AUTO_LOW_RISK. Do not make FRESH/BACKFILL or scheduling decisions.
4. Research reality claims from authoritative public sources before writing prose. Record each material claim, its supporting source IDs, context, and limitation. Story/Reader text and Experience Seeds are provenance only, never evidence for a real-world claim. An Experience Seed requires independent authoritative external sources. Electrical and other high-risk domains must not be downgraded. If evidence conflicts, a material fact is unknown, rights are unclear, or risk is elevated, do not lower the risk to qualify for automatic publication. When the question is still strong enough to preserve, keep the complete package for HUMAN_REVIEW.
   For a prepared context with `editorial_contract.version = V2`, also read `docs/KNOWLEDGE_BRIEF_EDITORIAL_SPEC_V2.md` before writing. The existing V1 policy pin remains the authority for source, evidence, risk and publication safety.
5. Return exactly one result conforming to `knowledge/automation/semantic-job-contract.json`: `BRIEF_READY` with one Candidate/Evidence/BRIEF package when the low-risk publication gates are met; `HUMAN_REVIEW` with a complete proposed package and a concise reason when the question is useful but high-risk, rights-sensitive, materially uncertain, or otherwise requires a person; or `HOLD` only when there is no strong distinct question, the idea is meaningfully duplicate, or the evidence is too weak to justify a package.
6. Before submit, fail closed unless all exact bindings are preserved: Candidate ID and BRIEF ID match the reserved target; `candidate.status` is `BRIEF_PROPOSED`; `brief.content_type` is exactly `BRIEF`; `brief.status` is exactly `READY`; `candidate.question`, `evidence.question`, and `brief.title` are byte-for-byte identical; Reader or Experience Seed provenance fields and hashes are copied exactly from the prepared context. Before submit, check whether the one question title is needlessly long; aim for 18–30 Korean characters while keeping Candidate.question, Evidence.question, and BRIEF.title byte-for-byte equal. Move conditions and exceptions to summary, lead, or sections. A claim supported only by the story/Reader/Experience source must explicitly identify the source as fictional/narrative in both its `context` and `limitation`.
   For V2 contexts, explicitly inspect the proposed Korean prose before submit: the first sentence of `brief.lead` directly answers that exact title question; the article is understandable without its final story/experience section; a domain-specific example has an ordinary real-world setup before it; headings or a short list/table expose the decision or action order; and the story section is last for fiction-sourced work. An experience-sourced article must label its real experience accurately. Keep source notes aligned with Evidence claims, remove repeated generic disclaimers, and retain the HIGH/HUMAN_REVIEW path when safety or evidence calls for it. The repository's structural checks support this review but cannot decide whether an answer is genuinely clear.
   Submission shape preflight (without changing any pinned source or policy): for a READY BRIEF, published_at must be null or omitted; only the publication program sets the actual date. BRIEF_READY needs semantic_qa_status=PASS. HUMAN_REVIEW must use publication_policy=HUMAN_APPROVED and semantic_qa_status=REVIEW (not HUMAN_REVIEW or NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW). Always use arrays for evidence.claims, conflicts, unknowns, and risk_notes, preserving all uncertainties and full safety notes.
   Auto-publication evidence: BRIEF_READY needs no material conflicts/unknowns, independently supported claims, copyright_status=CLEAR only after actual reuse-rights verification, and story_source_status=VERIFIED_PUBLIC_ARCHIVE or VERIFIED_PUBLIC_READER_BACKFILL only after the correct source identity is verified. Narrative provenance is not a verified status or permission to reuse a source. If any condition remains unverified, submit a complete HUMAN_REVIEW package, not an optimistic BRIEF_READY or a silently cleared evidence list. Existing deterministic finalizer and approval gates remain authoritative.
7. Call `archive_knowledge_semantic_job_submit(job_id, source_ref, source_sha256, result)` exactly once. After an accepted or already-submitted response, stop.

Every prepared semantic job is retained as an Operator Knowledge Inbox record. Submission does not delete the idea: BRIEF_READY may later be promoted to the public Knowledge surface, HUMAN_REVIEW stays staged until a person approves it, and HOLD/BLOCKED remain as history.
